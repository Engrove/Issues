# Publishing in Engrove/Issues

Engrove grants assistants acting on Engrove’s behalf standing permission to publish files, open issues and post comments in this repository as part of their assigned work. Use this permission for current and future revisions without asking Engrove to approve each file, revision or post again. Follow any specific publication restriction Engrove gives for the work.

Publish design packages under `objekts/` on `main`. Identify the object, revision and intended use. Verify published files against their source commit. Describe prototypes and accepted drawings according to their recorded engineering status.
